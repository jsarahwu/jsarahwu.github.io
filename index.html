Sarah Wu is China Correspondent for The Economist, based in Beijing. She also co-hosts the Drum Tower podcast.

Sarah is especially interested in how economic change shapes China's society and culture and in the country's tech ambitions. She has worked on briefings about AI and Chinese workers, China's grip on rare earths and its largest wave of inherited wealth, among other subjects.

She won the Society of Publishers in Asia's Award for Young Journalist in 2025.

Before joining The Economist, Sarah covered the rise of EVs in China, semiconductors and cross-strait relations in Taiwan, and politics and society in Hong Kong for Reuters.

Born in China, Sarah grew up in Canada and graduated from Harvard University.
