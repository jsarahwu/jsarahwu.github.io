Sarah Wu is a China Correspondent at The Economist, based in Beijing. She also co-hosts the Drum Tower podcast.

Sarah covers China’s society, economy and technology. She is especially interested in how the slowing economy is reshaping culture and society. One theme she writes about is the changing nature of work, including the impact of AI and automation, the ballooning gig economy and the first wave of inherited wealth. She received the Society of Publishers in Asia's Award for Young Journalist in 2026.

Before joining The Economist, Sarah covered the rise of electric vehicles in China, semiconductors and cross-strait relations in Taiwan, and politics and society in Hong Kong for Reuters. Born in China, Sarah grew up in Canada and graduated from Harvard University.
