Sarah Wu is a China Correspondent for The Economist, based in Beijing. She also co-hosts the Drum Tower podcast with Jeremy Page.

Sarah is especially interested in the country's tech ambitions and how China’s changing economy shapes its society and culture. She has worked on briefings about AI and Chinese workers, China's grip on rare earths and its largest wave of inherited wealth, among other subjects.

Before joining The Economist, Sarah covered the rise of EVs in China, semiconductors and cross-strait relations in Taiwan, and politics and society in Hong Kong for Reuters. Born in China, Sarah grew up in Canada and graduated from Harvard University.
