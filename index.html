Sarah Wu is a China Correspondent at The Economist, based in Beijing. She also co-hosts the Drum Tower podcast.

Sarah covers China’s culture, economy, politics, society and technology. She is especially interested in how new technologies and slowing growth are reshaping the lives of ordinary people. She often reports on the changing nature of work, from AI's impact and the ballooning gig economy to the first wave of inherited wealth. She received the Society of Publishers in Asia's Award for Young Journalist in 2026.

Before joining The Economist, Sarah covered the rise of electric vehicles in China, semiconductors and cross-strait relations in Taiwan, and politics and society in Hong Kong for Reuters. Born in China, Sarah grew up in Canada and graduated from Harvard University.
